# Three-platform mode matrix v1

Checked: 2026-11-01

## A — Douyin / 抖音

- **Entry:** personalized recommendation feed, search, profile and sharing surfaces.
- **Asset split:** uploaded video cover, actual video first frame and opening segment are three separate assets/experiences.
- **Creative job:** make the subject/action/result legible; make the cover promise match the opening and the whole video.
- **Signals disclosed publicly:** user-history/content-feature matching, time context, interaction-behavior prediction and diversity adjustment. Likes, saves, shares and comments are examples, not a published weight table.
- **Do not claim:** fixed initial traffic pool, fixed first-3-second threshold, fixed cover CTR weight.

## B — WeChat Channels / 微信视频号

- **Entry:** regulator disclosure confirms both friends recommendation and algorithm recommendation; also social sharing and profile surfaces.
- **Asset split:** vertical/horizontal video canvas and uploaded cover are distinct; inspect the current publish UI.
- **Creative job:** clear topic + recognizable creator/brand + credible evidence + share-context readability.
- **Do not claim:** friend/algorithm traffic percentages, fixed share thresholds, or that share count is a published ranking weight.
- **Boundary:** this is 视频号, not 公众号.

## C — Xiaohongshu / 小红书

- **Entry:** recommendation feed + active search.
- **Asset split:** image-note first image/cover and video-note cover are distinct.
- **Creative job:** query intent, note subject, cover/title/body consistency, useful detail and save-worthy value.
- **Research evidence:** Xiaohongshu papers discuss Explore Feed cascade ranking, search query-note relevance and note semantics; these are not creator-facing weight tables.
- **Do not claim:** fixed CES weights, keyword repetition boosts, universal traffic pools or a single mandatory ratio for every content type.

## Shared but isolated

Content type, audience, motive, commercial goal, evidence, layout families and visual DNA can be shared as a decision framework. Platform distribution, title fields, cover/first-frame relationship, ratio, CTA and post-publish metrics must remain platform-specific.
